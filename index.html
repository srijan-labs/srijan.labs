The challenge we address

Education for a new reality

In a world shaped by AI, climate risk, and social polarisation, young people need more than academic achievement.

They need the human capacities to see clearly, act with courage, collaborate with purpose, and design solutions for real problems — starting in their own communities.

Our answer: a framework for human-centric learning

To meet this challenge, we designed the SALD model — an experiential pathway that builds awareness, agency, leadership, and design capacity through real-world projects, guided reflection, and mentorship.

The SALD model (See, Act, Lead, Design)

A structured yet flexible framework that develops inner clarity and outer action, through hands-on, community-rooted experiences.

1. See

Awareness

Students slow down to notice self, others, and systems — developing language for emotions, patterns, and power.

2. Act

Agency

Students practice taking initiative, working with constraints, and staying with a problem long enough to learn from it.

3. Lead

Leadership

Students shift from “I can” to “we can” — learning to listen, hold space, and move groups towards shared intentions.

4. Design

Problem solving

Students design grounded solutions using human-centred tools, prototyping, feedback cycles, and thoughtful storytelling.

What makes our model unique

The work sits at the intersection of classroom practice, district-level systems, and global youth leadership — not as a camp, but as a repeatable model.

A proprietary 4-stage model

See–Act–Lead–Design builds capacities from the ground up instead of adding “leadership” as an extra session.

Bridging ecosystems

Students connect self, peers, and community — working with local people, places, and stories so their projects grow from real life, not hypotheticals.

Deeply immersive

Students live leadership — through field immersions, design labs, and art-based reflection — instead of only reading about it.

Global partnership

SEL-informed identity work with FacesUp Uganda brings a powerful cross-cultural lens and global solidarity to local projects.

Integrated meta-learning

Reflection, feedback, and meta-learning are woven through every stage — building the muscle to learn how to learn.

Mentor pods & expert sessions

Students learn in small mentor pods, supported by reflective circles and dialogues with real-world practitioners and artists.

From classroom to community

Our journey began as Teach For India Fellows, teaching in communities marked by socio-economic instability yet filled with raw talent and imagination.

That work grew into district- and system-level roles across thousands of schools. Through it all, one question persisted: in a world of AI and automation, what is the true purpose of education?

We realized the core of education lies in nurturing awareness, agency, and meta-learning.

This is transformational leadership.

An inner shift that enables anyone to lead change. Srijan Labs is our way of building a community that explores, practices, and shares that inner dimension — starting with young people, and rippling outward.
